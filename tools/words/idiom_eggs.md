# Idioms that most need an easter egg (shortlist)

Picked because the picture or the movement IS the meaning, and a kid would not guess it from the words.

## Tier 1: build a new egg (30)
| Idiom | Egg idea |
|---|---|
| Raining cats and dogs | DONE |
| Break the ice | tap the frozen pond: cracks spread, two people can shake hands |
| Let the cat out of the bag | tap the bag: the cat jumps out and runs |
| Spill the beans | tap the jar: beans pour across the page |
| Butterflies in my stomach | see inside a tummy: butterflies flutter harder as the test gets closer |
| Get cold feet | feet turn blue and shiver at the edge of the stage |
| See red | the screen floods red, steam from the ears |
| Cool as a cucumber | a cucumber in sunglasses stays calm while everything burns |
| Over the moon | drag a kid up and up until he jumps over the moon |
| Cost an arm and a leg | price tag grows; an arm and a leg pay for it |
| Once in a blue moon | a long wait, then a rare blue moon appears once |
| Kill two birds with one stone | throw one stone (Toss game) and hit two birds |
| Storm in a teacup | a tiny storm with lightning inside a teacup |
| Bite off more than you can chew | a giant burger; the bite does not fit |
| In hot water | a pot starts to boil with the kid in it |
| Pull someone's leg | a leg stretches like elastic, then "just joking!" |
| Hold your horses | horses charge; tap to make them stop |
| Bolt from the blue | a calm blue sky, then a sudden lightning bolt |
| Time flies | a clock grows wings and flies away |
| A fish out of water | a fish flops on dry land |
| Under the weather | a small grey cloud follows a sick kid |
| Don't put all your eggs in one basket | game: carry eggs; they all break if you use one basket |
| Burn the midnight oil | a lamp at night; the oil runs down while he studies |
| Snowed under | snow piles up until only a nose shows |
| Chase your tail | a dog spins and goes nowhere |
| Green with envy | a face slowly turns green |
| Head in the clouds | a kid floats up and the real world gets small |
| Red tape | red tape wraps a letter so it cannot leave |
| Tongue-tied | the tongue ties in a knot |
| Black sheep | a black sheep among white ones |

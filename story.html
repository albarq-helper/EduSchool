    How the Story builder works

    Build your own story from vocabulary words: pick them, put them in order, add your own words and scenes. Then send it to your parent, who turns it into a finished story and a movie.

    Levels

    Your parent chooses the level for each story. Higher levels ask for more writing.

    LevelNameWhat you must do

    How it works for you

    • Pick words from the Word library (tap a picture to watch and hear it), press + Add or drag them into the story, and put them in order. Add your own words and new scenes.
    • From level 4 up, a ✍️ Write your sentences section appears with a box for each scene, or for each word at levels 7–10.
    • Each box is checked while typing: is it long enough, does it use the word, does it start with a capital letter and end with . ! or ? A green ✓ appears when it's right, or a hint such as “use the word Kennel”.
    • A Ready to send? checklist shows what's still missing, and Send to parent stays blocked until everything is ticked.
    • My story so far shows your own sentences (or the words in order at levels 1–3).
    • If the story comes back with notes, a yellow box shows them. Fix the sentences, then press Send to parent again.

    What happens after you send it

    Your parent reads your story plan. If something needs fixing, your story comes back with notes in a yellow box. When it's ready, your parent turns it into a finished story, and then into a movie. You can read the story and watch the movie right here.

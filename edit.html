About this set

Units

Group terms and questions into units or chapters. Students can practise one unit or several.

Paste from a spreadsheet

Copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets and paste here. Terms: term · meaning · example · translation · unit · picture link. Questions: start the row with Q then question · choice A · choice B · choice C · choice D · correct letter · explanation · unit. True/false: start with TF then statement · TRUE or FALSE · explanation · unit.

Terms and questions

Word groupsGive each term a word type, themes and opposites. Words with the same group appear together on the Word list and in the Story builder. For similar words, type ≈ Huge (same group on each word). For opposites, type Hot vs. cold: hot. For a meter, type Hate ⇄ Love: +3 (−5 to +5) or Tiny → Gigantic: 4 (0 to 5).

Songs and texts

A unit can have several texts (a poem, script or reading) and several songs. Link a song to its text. Lyrics: one line per line. Vocabulary words in the text and lyrics are highlighted for students automatically, and become the gaps in “Fill in the words”.
